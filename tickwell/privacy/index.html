Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Short version: Tickwell works entirely on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share your tasks, lists or personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't ask for internet access.

Who we are

Tickwell is made by Arcova ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email arcova.apps@gmail.com.

Your tasks and lists

Everything you add to Tickwell (tasks, notes, projects, saved shopping lists, reminders and settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Tickwell has no servers and does not request internet access.

If you share text into Tickwell from another app, it becomes a task on your phone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

Backups and new phones

Android can include Tickwell's data in your phone's own backup (for example Google backup tied to your phone's Google account) and in phone-to-phone transfers such as Samsung Smart Switch. These are features of your phone, controlled by you in your phone's settings. We never see that data.

You can also save a backup file from Settings → Backup & new phone. The file is saved where you choose, and restoring it replaces what is on the phone only after you confirm.

Reminders

Tickwell asks for permission to show notifications so it can remind you about tasks, and it may ask you to allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Tickwell does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports.

Third parties

Tickwell contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It uses open-source libraries that run on your device and do not send data anywhere. Google Play may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download the app.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

Children

Tickwell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any task, list or project in the app, empty the Trash, or remove everything by uninstalling Tickwell or clearing its storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of Tickwell needs to handle data differently, for example optional sync, we will say so here before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email arcova.apps@gmail.com.
