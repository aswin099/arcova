What it does

Your day, your week, your shopping.

Open it and start. No sign-up, no setup.

Today and Tonight

See what's due now, with the evening kept separate. Tick it, swipe it to later, or drag to reorder.

Upcoming

Swipe through the weeks ahead or open the month calendar. Tap a day to plan it.

Saved shopping lists

Keep a monthly grocery list once. When something runs out, tap a day, start typing, and pick it. Tick items off in the shop.

Type it naturally

Reminders on time

Notifications arrive on the minute, with Done and "In 1 hour" right there. Repeat daily, weekly or monthly.

Widgets and shortcuts

Questions

Good to know.

Is Tickwell free?

Yes. Tickwell is free, with no ads and no sign-up.

How do I move my tasks to a new phone?

Set up the new phone with the same Google account and restore your apps, or use your phone maker's transfer tool such as Smart Switch. Tickwell comes along with your tasks and lists. You can also save a backup file in Settings → Backup & new phone and restore it on the new phone.

Why are some reminders a few minutes late?

On Android 12 and newer, allow Tickwell in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Alarms & reminders. Tickwell asks the first time you set a reminder.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8.0 and newer. An iPhone version is planned.

Who makes Tickwell, and how do I get help?

Tickwell is made by Arcova. Email arcova.apps@gmail.com with questions, problems or ideas. We usually reply within two working days.
