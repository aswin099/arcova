What it does

Everything you reach for, in one app.

Open a PDF and get on with it. No sign-up, no waiting for a server.

Organize pages

Drag to reorder, rotate, duplicate or delete pages. Pull out just the pages you need.

Merge and split

Combine several PDFs in the order you choose, or break one big file into smaller ones.

Fill and sign

Save your signature once, then add it to any PDF in one tap. Add your initials, the date or any text too.

Compress

Shrink big scans for email. See the exact new size before you save, and keep the text sharp.

Photos and PDFs

Turn photos into a PDF, save pages as JPG or PNG, or pull the text out of a PDF.

Handy converters

Length, weight, temperature, data and more, plus binary, hex and Roman numerals.

Private by design

Your files stay on your phone.

Inkly doesn't have a server to send your documents to. It works on your phone, and that is the whole idea.

  • No accountOpen the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, no trackingNo advertising or analytics software, and no data collected about you.
  • No internet neededThe Android app doesn't even ask for internet access, so it can't upload your files.
  • Signatures stay putSaved signatures live in private storage, left out of cloud backups.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Inkly free?

Yes. Inkly is free to use, with no ads and no sign-up.

Do my documents get uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are processed on your phone. They only leave it when you choose Share or Save to… and then they go only where you send them.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 7.0 and newer. An iPhone version is planned.

Can I open a PDF from WhatsApp or Gmail?

Yes. Choose Inkly from the "Open with" or Share menu and the PDF opens in Inkly. Share several at once and they open ready to merge.

I forgot a PDF's password. Can Inkly open it?

No. Inkly can save an unlocked copy of a PDF if you know the password, but it can't recover forgotten ones.

Who makes Inkly, and how do I get help?

Inkly is made by Arcova. Email arcova.apps@gmail.com with questions, problems or ideas. We usually reply within two working days.
