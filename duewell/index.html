Questions

Good to know.

Is Duewell free?

Yes. Duewell is free, with no ads and no sign-up. Reminders, the calendar, spend totals, widgets and backups are all free.

Does Duewell pay my bills or read my bank SMS?

No. You add what you pay, and Duewell reminds you. "Pay with UPI" just opens your own UPI app with the payee and amount filled in; you pay there, with your own PIN. Duewell never sees your bank, card or UPI details, and it doesn't read your messages.

What about auto-debits?

Turn on auto-debit for a due, like a home loan EMI, and Duewell counts it as paid on its date. You still get a heads-up before, so you can keep enough in the account.

How do I move my dues to a new phone?

Set up the new phone with the same Google account and restore your apps, or use your phone maker's transfer tool such as Smart Switch. Duewell comes along with your dues and history. You can also save a backup file in Settings and restore it on the new phone.

Why are some reminders a few minutes late?

On Android 12 and newer, allow Duewell in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Alarms & reminders. Duewell asks the first time you set a reminder.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8.0 and newer. An iPhone version is planned.
