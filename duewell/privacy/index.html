Duewell for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Short version: Duewell works entirely on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share your dues, payments or personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, doesn't read your messages, never asks for bank, card or UPI details, and doesn't ask for internet access.

Who we are

Duewell is made by Arcova ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email arcova.apps@gmail.com.

Your dues and payments

Everything you add to Duewell (the names and amounts of your dues, due dates, which months you marked paid, notes such as who you pay, optional UPI IDs, and your settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Duewell has no servers and does not request internet access.

Duewell does not read your SMS, emails, contacts, location or bank accounts. It only knows what you type into it.

Pay with UPI

If you save a payee's UPI ID, Duewell can open a UPI app already on your phone (such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm) with the payee, amount and a note filled in. You complete the payment in that app, under that app's own terms and privacy policy. Duewell never sees your UPI PIN, bank account or the result of the payment; it simply asks you afterwards whether it went through.

Backups and new phones

Android can include Duewell's data in your phone's own backup (for example Google backup tied to your phone's Google account) and in phone-to-phone transfers such as Samsung Smart Switch. These are features of your phone, controlled by you in your phone's settings. We never see that data.

You can also save a backup file from Settings. The file is saved where you choose, and restoring it replaces what is on the phone only after you confirm. Keep the file somewhere safe, as it contains your list of dues.

Reminders

Duewell asks for permission to show notifications so it can remind you before dues, and it may ask you to allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time. It also restarts your reminders after the phone restarts. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Duewell does not collect personal or financial information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports.

Third parties

Duewell contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It uses open-source libraries that run on your device and do not send data anywhere. Google Play may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download the app.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

Children

Duewell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any due in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling Duewell or clearing its storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. A future version of Duewell may offer an optional Arcova account to back up and sync your dues between phones. If it does, it will stay optional, and we will update this policy to explain exactly what is stored and where before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email arcova.apps@gmail.com.
