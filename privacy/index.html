Inkly

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Applies to the Inkly app on Android and iOS

Short version: Inkly works entirely on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share any of your files or personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and on Android it doesn't even request internet access.

Who we are

Inkly is made by Arcova ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email arcova.apps@gmail.com.

Your files

When you open, create or convert a file in Inkly, it is processed on your device. Files you open or create are kept in Inkly's private storage on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. Files leave your phone only when you choose Share or Save to…, and then they go only where you send them.

Photos and camera

Inkly uses the system photo picker and camera only when you choose to add photos, for example in Images to PDF. It sees only the photos you pick. Nothing is uploaded.

Passwords

If you remove a password from a PDF, you type it in to unlock the file on your device. Inkly never stores or sends it.

Data we collect

None. Inkly does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports. Your theme and appearance settings are stored only on your phone.

Third parties

Inkly contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It uses open-source libraries (listed in the app under Settings → Open-source licences) that run on your device and do not send data anywhere. Google Play and the App Store may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the app.

Children

Inkly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any file from the Files tab, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of Inkly needs to handle data differently, we will say so here before that version is released.
